The platform: AIRM is a complete, containerized AI Risk & Reliability Management platform and continuous telemetry underwriting engine. It pairs standard-library multi-format architecture intake (parsing 10 document formats including Word, Excel, DrawIO, Mermaid, and PDFs) with dynamic telemetry package synthesis cryptographically signed with HMAC-SHA256 digests by the central carrier authority (IC Cloud). Evaluated systems run an isolated aglet container executing signed packages locally—ensuring raw telemetry and cloud credentials (AWS, Datadog) stay strictly within company memory. Control screening is translated into score-only carrier exports, triggering closed-loop dynamic premium rate adjustments, FAIR-style Monte Carlo loss simulation, and multi-framework governance measurement across NIST AI RMF / Generative AI Profile, ISO 42001/42005/23894, and OWASP 2025.

Beta boundary & carrier roadmap: Alpha/beta participation is strictly limited to non-commercial individuals and eligible personal or community participants running complex, multi-agent, persistent architectures (e.g. OpenClaw). In parallel, the core platform is engineered for future carrier-facing distribution under the carrier's own white-label branding (Decision 16). Review eligibility and apply →
